Warnings

  • For external use only.
  • Do not use if you are pregnant.
  • Do not use on broken, cracked, bleeding or irritated skin.
  • Do not use if you're allergic to bitter melon or other gourds (cucumber, melon, squash, zucchini).
  • If you have diabetes or take medicine that lowers blood sugar, ask your doctor before use.
  • Avoid contact with eyes. If it gets in your eyes, rinse well with water.
  • Stop use and wash the area with water if irritation or a rash develops.
  • Keep out of reach of children. Store the bottle closed, at room temperature, away from direct sunlight.

Signs to call for help

If your baby vomits, seems unusually sleepy, floppy or hard to wake, looks pale or sweaty, or gets a rash or swelling after nursing, call your pediatrician or Poison Control at 1-800-222-1222 (free, 24/7, US). If baby has trouble breathing or won't wake up, call 911.

Weaning and your health

Hard, hot, red or painful areas on your breasts, or a fever, can mean mastitis or a blocked duct. Call your doctor. Weaning gradually lowers the risk.

Feeding your baby after weaning

Until 12 months, breast milk or infant formula is your baby's main food. If you wean from the breast before 12 months, talk to your pediatrician about infant formula. Don't give cow's milk as a drink before 12 months.

Report a problem

Had a reaction? Please tell us at hello@wallowbaby.com so we can look into it. You can also report reactions to cosmetic products to the FDA through MedWatch.
