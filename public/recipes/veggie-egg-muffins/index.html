Veggie egg muffins

Mini egg muffins with grated veggies and a little cheese: soft, portable and freezer-friendly.

Prep10 min
Cook15 min
Makes12 mini muffins

No honeybefore 12 months, not even baked in or on a pacifier. It can cause infant botulism.

Ingredients

  • 4 large eggs
  • 1/4 cup grated zucchini, squeezed dry
  • 1/4 cup finely chopped spinach
  • 2 tbsp grated low-sodium mozzarella
  • 1 tsp oil, for the pan

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Brush a 12-cup mini muffin pan with oil.
  2. Whisk the eggs, then stir in the zucchini, spinach and cheese.
  3. Divide among the muffin cups, filling each about three-quarters full.
  4. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes, until set all the way through and a toothpick comes out clean. Cool before serving.

Texture for 10 months+

Soft and springy, fully set in the middle. Offer whole for baby to bite or break into small soft pieces.

Choking check

  • Grate or finely chop all vegetables so there are no hard pieces.
  • Make sure the egg is fully cooked with no runny middle.

Always seat baby upright and stay with them while they eat.

Allergens

  • Egg: Make sure egg has been introduced before serving this recipe.
  • Milk: Mozzarella is dairy. Leave it out if your baby avoids dairy.

New allergen? Offer it at home, earlier in the day, one new allergen at a time, then watch your baby for 2 hours. Hives, vomiting, swelling of the face or lips, or trouble breathing: call 911.

Storage

Fridge up to 3 days, or freeze up to 2 months. Reheat until hot, then cool before serving.

Tips

  • Keep cheese to a small amount: most cheeses are salty.
  • Swap in grated carrot, finely chopped red pepper or mashed sweet potato.

Still under 12 months? Breast milk or infant formula is still your baby's main food. Solids add to milk, they don't replace it. Weaning from the breast before 12 months? Ask your pediatrician which infant formula to use.
