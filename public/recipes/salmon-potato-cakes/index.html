Mini salmon and potato cakes

Soft little fish cakes made with just salmon and potato, no flour or salt. Easy to pick up and easy to batch-freeze.

Prep10 min
Cook25 min
MakesAbout 10 mini cakes

No honeybefore 12 months, not even baked in or on a pacifier. It can cause infant botulism.

Ingredients

  • 1 medium potato (about 6 oz), peeled and cubed
  • 4 oz skinless, boneless salmon fillet
  • 1 tsp chopped fresh dill or a squeeze of lemon (optional)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil, for the pan

Method

  1. Boil the potato for 12 to 15 minutes until very soft. Drain and mash until smooth.
  2. Meanwhile, steam or poach the salmon for 8 to 10 minutes, until it flakes easily and is opaque all the way through (145°F / 63°C).
  3. Flake the salmon onto a plate and run your fingers through every piece to check for bones.
  4. Mix the salmon into the potato with the dill, if using. Shape into about 10 small cakes, around 2 inches wide.
  5. Warm the oil in a nonstick pan over medium-low heat and cook the cakes 3 minutes per side until lightly golden. Cool before serving.

Texture for 10 months+

Soft, moist cakes that break apart easily. Serve whole for baby to hold and bite, or break into small soft pieces about the size of a pea for pincer-grasp practice.

Choking check

  • Fish bones are the biggest risk here: check every flake with your fingers, even in 'boneless' fillets.
  • Keep the cakes soft. If the outside crisps hard, break them open and offer the soft inside.

Always seat baby upright and stay with them while they eat.

Allergens

  • Fish: Fish is a common allergen. If salmon is new, offer a small taste of plain cooked salmon first. Salmon is one of the best fish choices for babies because it's low in mercury.

New allergen? Offer it at home, earlier in the day, one new allergen at a time, then watch your baby for 2 hours. Hives, vomiting, swelling of the face or lips, or trouble breathing: call 911.

Storage

Fridge up to 2 days. Freeze cooked, cooled cakes on a tray, then bag them, up to 1 month. Reheat until hot all the way through, then cool before serving.

Tips

  • Use leftover cooked salmon from a family dinner, as long as it was cooked without salt or spicy seasoning.
  • Swap the potato for sweet potato for a sweeter, more colorful cake.
