Easy baby hummus

Smooth, mild hummus with no added salt: great on a spoon, and a simple way to introduce sesame.

Prep10 min
Cook0 min
MakesAbout 1 cup

No honeybefore 12 months, not even baked in or on a pacifier. It can cause infant botulism.

Ingredients

  • 1 can (15 oz) no-salt-added chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1 tbsp tahini (sesame paste), well stirred
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 2 to 4 tbsp water

Method

  1. Put everything except the water in a blender or food processor.
  2. Blend, adding water a tablespoon at a time, until smooth and creamy.
  3. Taste and adjust: no salt needed for baby. Scoop out baby's portion, then season the rest for grown-ups if you like.

Texture for 8 months+

Thick and smooth, slightly lumpy is fine at 8 months. Serve on a pre-loaded spoon or spread thinly on a soft toast strip.

Choking check

  • Blend until no whole chickpeas or skins remain: whole chickpeas are a choking risk.
  • Spread thinly. Thick dollops of any sticky spread can be hard for babies to swallow.

Always seat baby upright and stay with them while they eat.

Allergens

  • Sesame: Tahini is made from sesame, one of the top 9 allergens. If sesame is new, offer a small amount on its own first and watch your baby for 2 hours.

New allergen? Offer it at home, earlier in the day, one new allergen at a time, then watch your baby for 2 hours. Hives, vomiting, swelling of the face or lips, or trouble breathing: call 911.

Storage

Fridge up to 3 days in a sealed container. Freeze in small portions up to 1 month; stir well after thawing.

Tips

  • Spread on a strip of soft toast for a finger food (this adds wheat).
  • Stir a spoonful into pureed roasted red pepper or carrot for color.

Still under 12 months? Breast milk or infant formula is still your baby's main food. Solids add to milk, they don't replace it. Weaning from the breast before 12 months? Ask your pediatrician which infant formula to use.
