Soft banana oat fingers

Two-ingredient baked oat fingers that are soft enough for new eaters and perfect for little hands.

Prep10 min
Cook20 min
MakesAbout 12 fingers

No honeybefore 12 months, not even baked in or on a pacifier. It can cause infant botulism.

Ingredients

  • 2 very ripe bananas
  • 1 cup quick oats
  • 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon (optional)

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a small baking dish (about 8 x 8 in) with parchment.
  2. Mash the bananas well, then stir in the oats and cinnamon. Let it sit 5 minutes so the oats soften.
  3. Press the mixture into the dish about 1/2 inch thick.
  4. Bake for 18 to 20 minutes until just set. Cool completely, then cut into finger-size strips.

Texture for 8 months+

Soft and moist, like a dense banana bread. A finger should squash easily when pressed.

Choking check

  • Don't overbake: dry, crunchy edges are harder for babies to manage. Trim them off if they crisp.
  • Use quick oats so the fingers hold together softly.

Always seat baby upright and stay with them while they eat.

Allergens

None of the top 9 allergens. A gentle pick for days you're not introducing anything new.

Storage

Fridge up to 3 days in a sealed container, or freeze individually up to 1 month.

Tips

  • Oats are naturally gluten-free but are often processed with wheat; choose certified gluten-free oats if you need to.
  • Stir in 2 tbsp grated apple or mashed berries for variety.
