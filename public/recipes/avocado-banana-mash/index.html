Avocado and banana mash

A no-cook, two-ingredient first food: creamy, mild and ready in five minutes.

Prep5 min
Cook0 min
Makes2 to 3 baby portions

No honeybefore 12 months, not even baked in or on a pacifier. It can cause infant botulism.

Ingredients

  • 1/4 ripe avocado
  • 1/4 small ripe banana (brown spots are good)
  • 1 to 2 tsp breast milk or prepared infant formula, to thin (optional)

Method

  1. Wash your hands. Scoop the avocado flesh into a small bowl.
  2. Add the banana and mash both with a fork until completely smooth. For an extra-smooth first taste, press it through a fine sieve or use a small blender.
  3. Stir in breast milk or formula a little at a time until it drops easily off a spoon.
  4. Offer a few small spoonfuls. Let baby lean in for the spoon, and stop when they turn away.

Texture for 6 months+

Very smooth, like thick yogurt, with no lumps for first tastes. Thin with a little breast milk or formula if it's too thick. Once baby handles it easily, leave it slightly thicker.

Choking check

  • Mash until there are no firm pieces of avocado left; under-ripe avocado stays in hard chunks, so only use one that dents when you press it.
  • Serve on a spoon or as a thin smear on a pre-loaded spoon, not as cubes, at this stage.

Always seat baby upright and stay with them while they eat.

Allergens

None of the top 9 allergens. A gentle pick for days you're not introducing anything new.

Storage

Best made fresh: avocado and banana turn brown fast. Cover the leftover with plastic wrap pressed onto the surface and use within a few hours (fridge). Don't freeze.

Tips

  • Brown spots on the banana mean it's sweeter and softer, so it mashes smoother.
  • Want to serve it baby-led? Offer a ripe avocado spear about the length of an adult finger instead, rolled in a little baby oat cereal for grip.

Still under 12 months? Breast milk or infant formula is still your baby's main food. Solids add to milk, they don't replace it. Weaning from the breast before 12 months? Ask your pediatrician which infant formula to use.
