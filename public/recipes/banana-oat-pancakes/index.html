Banana oat pancakes with smashed blueberries

Three-ingredient pancakes sweetened only with banana: a breakfast the whole family can share.

Prep5 min
Cook10 min
MakesAbout 8 small pancakes

Ingredients

  • 1 ripe banana
  • 1 large egg
  • 1/2 cup quick oats, blended until fine
  • 2 to 3 tbsp whole milk, if the batter is too thick (optional)
  • 1/4 cup blueberries, smashed with a fork
  • 1 tsp oil, for the pan

Method

  1. Mash the banana in a bowl, then whisk in the egg.
  2. Stir in the blended oats. Let the batter rest 5 minutes to thicken; add a splash of milk if it's too thick to pour.
  3. Warm a little oil in a nonstick pan over medium-low heat. Spoon in small pancakes, about 2 tablespoons each.
  4. Cook 2 minutes until bubbles form, flip, and cook 1 to 2 minutes more, until cooked through in the middle.
  5. Cool slightly, cut into strips or small pieces, and top with the smashed blueberries.

Texture for 12 months+

Soft, fluffy small pancakes, cut into strips or small pieces. Blueberries are smashed flat so there are no whole round berries.

Choking check

  • Whole blueberries are a round-food choking risk for young toddlers: smash or quarter them.
  • Cut pancakes into strips or small pieces and skip syrup, which makes pieces stick together.

Always seat baby upright and stay with them while they eat.

Allergens

  • Egg: Make sure egg has been introduced before serving this recipe.
  • Milk (if used): Whole cow's milk is fine in food and as a drink from 12 months. Leave it out or use water if your child avoids dairy.

New allergen? Offer it at home, earlier in the day, one new allergen at a time, then watch your baby for 2 hours. Hives, vomiting, swelling of the face or lips, or trouble breathing: call 911.

Storage

Fridge up to 2 days, or freeze with parchment between pancakes up to 1 month. Reheat in a toaster or pan, then cool before serving.

Tips

  • Oats are naturally gluten-free but are often processed alongside wheat. Choose certified gluten-free oats if your child needs to avoid gluten.
  • Honey is safe from 12 months, but these don't need it: ripe banana does the sweetening.
