Spinach omelet strips

Soft, fully cooked egg strips with hidden greens: an easy way to keep egg in baby's week once it's been introduced.

Prep5 min
Cook6 min
Makes1 omelet, about 6 strips

No honeybefore 12 months, not even baked in or on a pacifier. It can cause infant botulism.

Ingredients

  • 1 large egg
  • 1 small handful baby spinach, very finely chopped
  • 1 tsp olive oil or avocado oil

Method

  1. Whisk the egg well in a small bowl, then stir in the finely chopped spinach.
  2. Warm the oil in a small nonstick pan over medium-low heat.
  3. Pour in the egg and swirl to make a thin layer. Cook 2 to 3 minutes until the edges set.
  4. Flip and cook another 2 to 3 minutes, until there is no runny or glossy egg anywhere.
  5. Let it cool, then cut into finger-length strips. Check the temperature in the middle before serving.

Texture for 8 months+

Soft and springy, fully set all the way through, cut into strips about the length and width of an adult finger so baby can hold one end in their fist. It should squash flat when you press it.

Choking check

  • Chop the spinach very finely: whole leaves can stick to the roof of baby's mouth.
  • Cook the egg until completely firm. Runny egg isn't recommended for babies.

Always seat baby upright and stay with them while they eat.

Allergens

  • Egg: Egg is one of the most common food allergens. If it's baby's first time, start with a small amount of plain, well-cooked egg on its own, then wait to see how they do before using this recipe.

New allergen? Offer it at home, earlier in the day, one new allergen at a time, then watch your baby for 2 hours. Hives, vomiting, swelling of the face or lips, or trouble breathing: call 911.

Storage

Fridge up to 2 days in a sealed container. Warm gently and check it isn't hot in the middle. Freeze cooled strips up to 1 month.

Tips

  • Swap spinach for finely grated zucchini (squeeze out the water first) or mashed sweet potato.
  • Once egg is in baby's diet, keep offering it regularly so it stays part of their routine.

Still under 12 months? Breast milk or infant formula is still your baby's main food. Solids add to milk, they don't replace it. Weaning from the breast before 12 months? Ask your pediatrician which infant formula to use.
