Beef and sweet potato mash

Tender ground beef mashed into sweet potato: a soft, iron-rich meal that's easy to batch cook.

Prep10 min
Cook25 min
MakesAbout 2 cups (6 to 8 baby portions)

No honeybefore 12 months, not even baked in or on a pacifier. It can cause infant botulism.

Ingredients

  • 4 oz lean ground beef
  • 1 medium sweet potato, peeled and cubed
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • 1/2 cup water

Method

  1. Steam or boil the sweet potato for 12 to 15 minutes until very soft.
  2. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a pan and cook the beef over medium heat, breaking it up into the smallest crumbs you can, for 6 to 8 minutes until no pink remains (160°F / 71°C).
  3. Add the water to the pan and simmer for 5 minutes to make the beef extra tender.
  4. Mash the sweet potato and stir in the beef with its cooking liquid. Pulse briefly in a food processor for a finer texture.

Texture for 8 months+

Lumpy and soft, like a thick mash with small, tender pieces of beef. Blend briefly for a smoother texture if baby is still getting used to lumps.

Choking check

  • Break the beef into very fine crumbs. Larger chunks of meat are hard for babies to chew.
  • Simmering in water keeps the meat moist; dry meat crumbs are harder to swallow.

Always seat baby upright and stay with them while they eat.

Allergens

None of the top 9 allergens. A gentle pick for days you're not introducing anything new.

Storage

Fridge up to 2 days in a sealed container, or freeze in an ice-cube tray, then bag the cubes, up to 1 month. Thaw in the fridge and warm gently. Stir and check the temperature before serving.

Tips

  • Red meat is one of the best sources of iron for babies.
  • Swap the beef for ground turkey or chicken thigh.
