Mini pasta with lentil tomato sauce

Tiny pasta in a smooth, salt-free tomato and lentil sauce that the whole family can eat.

Prep10 min
Cook25 min
MakesAbout 4 baby portions (and sauce to spare)

No honeybefore 12 months, not even baked in or on a pacifier. It can cause infant botulism.

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup dried red lentils, rinsed
  • 1 can (14 oz) no-salt-added crushed tomatoes
  • 1/2 small onion, finely grated
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1/2 cup small pasta shapes (like orzo, stelline or small shells)

Method

  1. Warm the oil in a saucepan and cook the grated onion for 3 minutes until soft.
  2. Add the lentils, tomatoes and water. Simmer, stirring now and then, for 18 to 20 minutes until the lentils are soft. Blend until smooth.
  3. Meanwhile, cook the pasta in unsalted water for 2 to 3 minutes longer than the package says. Drain.
  4. Stir 2 to 3 tablespoons of sauce into a small portion of pasta. Cool before serving.

Texture for 10 months+

Small, soft-cooked pasta shapes coated in a smooth sauce. Cook the pasta a few minutes past al dente so it squashes easily.

Choking check

  • Use small shapes and cook them extra soft.
  • Grate the onion so there are no firm pieces in the sauce.

Always seat baby upright and stay with them while they eat.

Allergens

  • Wheat: Most pasta is made from wheat. Introduce wheat in a simple form (like soft toast or plain pasta) first if it's new.

New allergen? Offer it at home, earlier in the day, one new allergen at a time, then watch your baby for 2 hours. Hives, vomiting, swelling of the face or lips, or trouble breathing: call 911.

Storage

Sauce keeps 3 days in the fridge or 2 months frozen. Cook pasta fresh, or store cooked pasta in the fridge for 2 days.

Tips

  • Season the grown-ups' portion after you've taken baby's out.
  • Use chickpea or lentil pasta for extra protein (check for allergens).
