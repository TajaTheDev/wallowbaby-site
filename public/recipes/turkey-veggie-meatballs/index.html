Soft turkey and veggie meatballs

Tender baked meatballs packed with grated veggies, cut into quarters for little eaters.

Prep15 min
Cook20 min
MakesAbout 20 small meatballs

Ingredients

  • 8 oz ground turkey
  • 1/4 cup grated zucchini, squeezed dry
  • 1/4 cup grated carrot
  • 1/4 cup quick oats
  • 1/2 tsp dried oregano

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C) and line a baking tray with parchment.
  2. Mix everything together with clean hands until combined.
  3. Roll into small balls, about 1 inch wide, and place on the tray.
  4. Bake for 18 to 20 minutes until cooked through (165°F / 74°C). Cool, then cut into quarters before serving.

Texture for 12 months+

Soft and moist. Cut each meatball into quarters or small pieces, or flatten slightly so it squashes easily.

Choking check

  • Cut meatballs into quarters: whole round meatballs are a choking risk for toddlers.
  • Don't overbake: dry meat is harder to chew and swallow.

Always seat baby upright and stay with them while they eat.

Allergens

None of the top 9 allergens. A gentle pick for days you're not introducing anything new.

Storage

Fridge up to 3 days, or freeze cooked meatballs up to 2 months. Reheat until hot through, then cool.

Tips

  • Serve with the lentil tomato sauce from our 10-month pasta recipe.
  • Season the grown-ups' portion separately.
