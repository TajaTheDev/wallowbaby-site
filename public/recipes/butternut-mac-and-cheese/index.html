Butternut squash mac and cheese

Creamy mac and cheese with hidden butternut squash and less cheese than the boxed kind.

Prep10 min
Cook25 min
MakesAbout 4 toddler portions

Ingredients

  • 1 cup small macaroni or shells
  • 1 cup peeled, cubed butternut squash
  • 1/2 cup whole milk
  • 1/3 cup grated mild cheddar
  • 1 tsp unsalted butter

Method

  1. Steam or boil the squash for 12 to 15 minutes until very soft.
  2. Cook the pasta in unsalted water until soft, then drain.
  3. Blend the squash with the milk until smooth, then warm it in the pasta pot with the butter.
  4. Stir in the cheese until melted, then add the pasta. Cool before serving.

Texture for 12 months+

Soft, small pasta shapes in a smooth sauce. Cook the pasta until very soft.

Choking check

  • Use small pasta shapes and cook them soft.
  • Let it cool and stir well: melted cheese can stay very hot in the middle.

Always seat baby upright and stay with them while they eat.

Allergens

  • Wheat: Regular macaroni is made from wheat.
  • Milk: Contains milk, cheese and butter. Whole cow's milk is fine in food and as a drink from 12 months.

New allergen? Offer it at home, earlier in the day, one new allergen at a time, then watch your baby for 2 hours. Hives, vomiting, swelling of the face or lips, or trouble breathing: call 911.

Storage

Fridge up to 3 days. Reheat with a splash of milk, stirring well, and check the temperature.

Tips

  • Swap squash for sweet potato or cauliflower.
  • Cheese is salty, so a little goes a long way for toddlers.
