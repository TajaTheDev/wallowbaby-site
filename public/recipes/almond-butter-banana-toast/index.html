Almond butter banana toast fingers

Soft toast with a thin layer of almond butter and mashed banana, cut into fingers for a quick breakfast.

Prep5 min
Cook2 min
Makes1 toddler portion

Ingredients

  • 1 slice whole-wheat bread
  • 1 tsp smooth almond butter, thinned with 1 tsp warm water
  • 1/4 ripe banana, mashed

Method

  1. Lightly toast the bread so it's warm but still soft. Remove the crusts if they're hard.
  2. Stir the almond butter with warm water until smooth and spreadable.
  3. Spread a very thin layer of almond butter over the toast, then top with mashed banana.
  4. Cut into finger-size strips.

Texture for 12 months+

Lightly toasted (not crunchy) bread, spread very thinly, cut into finger-size strips.

Choking check

  • Spread nut butter in a thin layer only. Thick nut butter can stick to the roof of the mouth and block the airway.
  • Never give whole nuts to children under 4.

Always seat baby upright and stay with them while they eat.

Allergens

  • Tree nuts: Almond is a tree nut, one of the top 9 allergens. If almond is new, offer a small amount of thinned almond butter first and watch for 2 hours.
  • Wheat: Contains wheat bread.

New allergen? Offer it at home, earlier in the day, one new allergen at a time, then watch your baby for 2 hours. Hives, vomiting, swelling of the face or lips, or trouble breathing: call 911.

Storage

Best made fresh.

Tips

  • Smooth peanut butter or sunflower seed butter work the same way.
  • Top with a sprinkle of ground flaxseed for extra fiber.
