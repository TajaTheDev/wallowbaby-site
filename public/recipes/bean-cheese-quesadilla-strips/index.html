Bean and cheese quesadilla strips

Mashed black beans and melty cheese in a soft tortilla, cut into easy-to-hold strips.

Prep5 min
Cook6 min
Makes1 to 2 toddler portions

Ingredients

  • 1 small whole-wheat tortilla
  • 2 tbsp no-salt-added black beans, rinsed and mashed
  • 2 tbsp grated mild cheddar or mozzarella
  • Pinch of ground cumin (optional)

Method

  1. Spread the mashed beans over half the tortilla. Sprinkle with cumin and cheese.
  2. Fold the tortilla in half and press gently.
  3. Cook in a dry nonstick pan over medium-low heat for 2 to 3 minutes per side, until golden and the cheese has melted.
  4. Cool for a few minutes, then cut into strips.

Texture for 12 months+

Soft and melty, cut into strips about the width of two fingers. Mash the beans so there are no whole beans.

Choking check

  • Mash the beans well: whole beans can be a choking risk for young toddlers.
  • Cool before serving; melted cheese stays very hot.

Always seat baby upright and stay with them while they eat.

Allergens

  • Wheat: Tortillas are made from wheat (choose corn tortillas if your child avoids wheat).
  • Milk: Contains cheese.

New allergen? Offer it at home, earlier in the day, one new allergen at a time, then watch your baby for 2 hours. Hives, vomiting, swelling of the face or lips, or trouble breathing: call 911.

Storage

Best fresh. Leftover strips keep 1 day in the fridge; reheat in a dry pan.

Tips

  • Add mashed avocado or a spoon of plain yogurt for dipping.
  • Mashed sweet potato instead of beans makes a sweeter version.
