Ingredients

What's inside

Three ingredients, and why each one is there.

Ingredient 1

Momordica charantia (bitter melon) tincture

Bitter melon, also called bitter gourd or karela, is a green, bumpy vegetable cooked across Asia, Africa and the Caribbean. It's one of the most bitter foods people eat, and that bitterness is the whole point of Wallow: it's what gives the serum its strong taste.

[PLACEHOLDER: confirm with the manufacturer] How the tincture is made (the extraction liquid, for example alcohol or glycerin) and its strength. If the tincture contains alcohol, alcohol must be listed in the ingredients on the label and here.

Ingredient 2

Distilled water

Purified water that forms the base of the serum so it spreads in a thin, even layer.

Ingredient 3

Vegetable glycerin

A plant-derived liquid used in skincare to help keep skin from drying out and give the serum a smooth feel.

Who shouldn't use it

Don't use Wallow if you're pregnant, if you're allergic to bitter melon or other gourds (like cucumber, melon or squash), or on broken, cracked or bleeding skin. If you have diabetes or take medicine that lowers blood sugar, check with your doctor first, because bitter melon is known to lower blood sugar when eaten. See the full safety page.
