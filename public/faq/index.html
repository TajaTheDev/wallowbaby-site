Everything moms ask us about Wallow and weaning.

What is Wallow Baby Weaner Serum?

A bitter-tasting topical made with bitter melon (Momordica charantia) tincture, distilled water and vegetable glycerin. It comes in a 0.5 fl oz amber dropper bottle and is designed to make nursing less appealing while you wean.

How does it work?

By taste. Bitter melon is extremely bitter, and most babies don't like bitter tastes, so nursing becomes less interesting. It works best as part of a gradual weaning plan.

Does it change my milk supply?

No. Wallow works by taste and doesn't change how much milk you make. Your supply comes down naturally as you nurse less.

When should I wean?

That's your family's decision. Many health organizations recommend breast milk or formula as baby's main food until 12 months, alongside solid foods from about 6 months. If you wean before 12 months, ask your pediatrician about infant formula.

Can I use it while pregnant?

No. Don't use Wallow during pregnancy.
